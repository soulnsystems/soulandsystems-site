About

I built the system I needed and couldn't find.

Two years into my tech career, I was doing everything the job-search playbook told me to: mass applications, generic templates, forcing confidence I didn't feel in interviews. It got me nowhere, and worse, I didn't recognize the person showing up to those interviews.

Then I stopped trying to perform a version of myself I thought companies wanted, and started building an actual system: clear positioning by reminding myself of my unique value and things I would not trade for anything, deliberate outreach, interview prep that let me show up as myself. Within 2 months I landed my dream job, I had defined what I wanted, what I would not compromise on, and for the first time, I had an interview with no fear but authenticity and confidence.

Once I saw the method work, I noticed it wasn't really about career at all — it was about how to build toward anything without abandoning yourself along the way. I started using the same structure for training consistently, brand growth and visibility and it worked there too. That's where the idea for Soul & Systems was born.

Based in Lyon. When I'm not coaching, you'll find me in the tech world or swimming.

What I believe

Three things this is built on.

Values aren't the obstacle

Growth that requires abandoning who you are doesn't last. It's not more disciplined to ignore your values — it's just a slower way to burn out.

Systems beat motivation

Motivation is a mood. A system is a structure that still works on the days motivation doesn't show up.

The method is portable

The same process that gets you a job offer will get you through a training block or a content plan. The goal changes. The method doesn't.
